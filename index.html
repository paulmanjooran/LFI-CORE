ATNM-DEMO-1001 — Slide Navigator
Slide 1 of 3
Mockup uses sample slides. The real Slides button shows that alert's own Google Slides file.

Glass: one set of settings (blur 14px, 64% white tint, soft edge and shadow) used by every window, so the whole app can share it. The popup fills about 95% of your browser window, so photos, the three text sections and the buttons are all visible together with no scrollbar. Only a very long text gets a slim scrollbar inside its own column (tick "Show a very long alert"). Photo holders are landscape (16:9) and always show the whole photo. LFI Video is dark green. Style: fluid blobs (life), glass (structure), candy gloss (buttons). Blobs drift only on the idle Hub and inside the glass windows, and pause while a popup is open, to stay light on phones; text always sits on a calm frosted tile. Docked card = the other window, parked top-left; tap it to swap. One ✕ (or Esc) closes both and returns to the Hub. On a phone there is no room for a corner card, so one full-screen window with a Details | Slides switch does the same job.
